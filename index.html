The Secret Bond & Quantum Entanglement

Leo and Mia live 9,560 kilometers apart across continents and oceans. However, they share a secret bond: they do not act fully independently of each other. They are entangled across this vast distance.

How it works under the hood:

  • We have six qubits in an entangled quantum graph state (3 qubits for Leo in London, 3 qubits for Mia in Tokyo).
  • Three qubits each allow 8 distinct nocturnal activities (2³ = 8): 000 for in bed, 001 for thinking, 010 for reading, 011 for listening to music, 100 for playing a game, 101 for cooking, 110 for watering plants, and 111 for stargazing.
  • Because their qubits are entangled across this vast distance, their activities correlate. Every new investigation assigns a hidden quantum state archetype with its own coupling pattern and drawn questions!
  • Realistic Quantum Uncertainty: Real quantum hardware exhibits physical measurement readout noise (~94% correlation fidelity), meaning quantum correlations are never 100% textbook absolutes—which is why your Field Notebook investigates patterns that occur almost always or almost never!

An Example: The Bell State

To illustrate what we mean by entanglement, consider the classic two-qubit Bell state:

|Φ⁺⟩ = (|00⟩ + |11⟩) / √2

In this Bell state, measuring either qubit produces 0 or 1 with equal probability. But notice:

  • When 0 occurs on the first qubit, you are certain that the other will be 0.
  • When 1 occurs on the first qubit, you are certain that the other will be 1.

Before measurement, neither qubit had a fixed value, yet measuring one instantly determines the other!

Multi-Qubit Entanglement & Probabilities

For multi-qubit systems, entanglement produces richer joint distributions. For example, in a three-qubit system, observing 01 on the first two qubits might mean there is a 75% chance the third qubit will be 1 and a 25% chance that it will be 0.

In Entangled Love, their 6-qubit quantum graph state entangles their individual nocturnal activities across 9,560 kilometers, giving rise to subtle, romantic quantum synchronicities you can deduce evening by evening.
