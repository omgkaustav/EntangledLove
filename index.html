Field Investigator's Guide

The Quantum Bond

Leo (in London) and Mia (in Tokyo) are separated by 9,560 km. Every evening, their routines are governed by a 4-qubit entangled graph state.

Leo's Room (London): Qubits 0 & 1
  • 00: Resting in bed
  • 01: Thinking of Mia
  • 10: Playing a game
  • 11: Cooking in kitchen
Mia's Room (Tokyo): Qubits 2 & 3
  • 00: Resting in bed
  • 01: Thinking of Leo
  • 10: Playing a game
  • 11: Cooking in kitchen

The Physics

Qubits 0 and 2 are coupled strongly with a quantum relationship operation (target 0.85). This means their high bits tend to agree: when one rests (bed/thinking), the other usually rests; when one is active (gaming/cooking), the other is usually active.

How to Play

  • Press Z or click left window to turn on Leo's light.
  • Press X or click right window to turn on Mia's light.
  • Press C to turn both lights on at once.
  • Press N to advance to the next evening.
  • Mark your deductions as ✓ True or ✗ False in the Field Notebook.
  • Observe as many evenings as you want! When you are confident, click SEAL OBSERVATIONS to score your accuracy.

Browser CORS & Proxy Guide

Browsers enforce Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS). External APIs like Moth Atlas require a proxy when accessed directly from client-side browser JavaScript.

1. Zero Setup Option (Recommended)

Use Local Simulator Mode. It runs purely client-side, reproduces the exact 4-qubit entanglement statistics, costs 0 credits, and works anywhere (including GitHub Pages).

2. Local Proxy (Already Running!)

A Python proxy is included in this repository. Simply click "Use Local Proxy (localhost:8787)" in Settings.

3. Vercel Edge Proxy

If deployed to Vercel, the included api/[...path].js route proxies API calls seamlessly with zero CORS issues.
