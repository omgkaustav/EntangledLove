Field Investigator's Guide

Leo lives in London. Mia lives in Tokyo. Across 9,560 km of oceans and continents, their hearts share a quiet, unspoken rhythm under the night sky.

Two Night-time Moods

Each evening, both Leo and Mia fall into one of two moods:

🌙 Resting Mood (50% of nights) Either resting in bed or thinking of the other.
✨ Active Mood (50% of nights) Either playing a video game or cooking dinner.

The Quantum Bond (A Pure Bell State)

Their evenings are bound together by an exact quantum Bell state:

  • Synchronized Moods: When Leo is in his resting mood, Mia is always in her resting mood. When Leo is active, Mia is always active.
  • Never Mismatched: You will never see one resting in bed while the other is cooking or playing games.
  • Gentle Freedom: Within their shared mood, what they specifically do (bed vs thinking, gaming vs cooking) is a soft 50/50 coin flip.

How to Observe & Deduce

  • Press Z to toggle Leo's light in London.
  • Press X to toggle Mia's light in Tokyo.
  • Press C to toggle both lights together.
  • Press N to turn the page to the next evening.
  • Mark each deduction in your Field Notebook as ✓ True or ✗ False.
  • Take as many evenings as you need to be sure! When you are ready, click SEAL OBSERVATIONS to reveal your score.

Browser CORS & Proxy Guide

Browsers enforce Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS). External APIs like Moth Atlas require a proxy when accessed directly from client-side browser JavaScript.

1. Zero Setup Option (Recommended)

Use Local Simulator Mode. It runs purely client-side, reproduces the exact 4-qubit entanglement statistics, costs 0 credits, and works anywhere (including GitHub Pages).

2. Local Proxy (Already Running!)

A Python proxy is included in this repository. Simply click "Use Local Proxy (localhost:8787)" in Settings.

3. Vercel Edge Proxy

If deployed to Vercel, the included api/[...path].js route proxies API calls seamlessly with zero CORS issues.
