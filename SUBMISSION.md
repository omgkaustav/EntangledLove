### Project description *
*Briefly explain your project’s concept and what artifacts it produced. What’s the motivation behind your project? 100–200 words.*

Entangled Love is an observational quantum indie game portraying two long-distance lovers separated by 9,560 km: Leo in London and Mia in Tokyo. Every evening, each flat’s window reveals a nocturnal activity (such as reading, stargazing, listening to music, or cooking). Beneath the diegetic narrative, both windows are physical halves of a single computational-basis measurement on a 6-qubit quantum graph state.

The project produces a fully playable, tactile web experience featuring hand-crafted dual-city visuals, dynamic lighting, an investigator's field logbook with graphite ticks and rubber-stamp grading, ambient audio loops, and an observational history inspector with an empirical 8×8 correlation matrix.

Our motivation was to demystify quantum entanglement by transforming abstract Bell inequalities into an emotional, human story. In quantum mechanics, neither partner has a predetermined classical activity until measured, yet their outcomes remain intrinsically correlated across vast distances. By stepping into the shoes of a nocturnal observer piecing together qualitative correlation statements across days, players develop authentic physical intuition for quantum measurement, entanglement, and readout noise without needing formal mathematical training.

### Technical description *
*Briefly describe the tools and techniques used to create the work—including which Moth Atlas engines were used. 50–100 words.*

Built using vanilla HTML5, CSS3, ES modules, and the Web Audio API without heavy framework dependencies. Quantum state generation connects to Moth Quantum's Atlas `graph-v1` engine, encoding 6 qubits (3 for Leo, 3 for Mia) across 8 nocturnal activities with 1,024-shot batches. The system supports live dispatch to real IBM QPUs and the Moth Cloud Emulator, alongside a seeded Mulberry32 local quantum simulator fallback. Network communication utilizes a lightweight Vercel Edge proxy (`api/[...path].js`) and local Python/Node proxies to eliminate browser CORS friction securely without leaking API credentials.

### QPU or emulation? *
*Does your project use emulation, or actual quantum hardware (QPUs)?*

Both QPU and emulation. 

The application provides a seamless toggle in the top-right Settings modal:
1. **Real Quantum Hardware (`qpu`)**: Dispatches jobs directly to physical IBM Quantum Processing Units via the Moth Atlas `graph-v1` pipeline, complete with job status polling and an abort controller.
2. **Cloud Emulator (`emu`)**: Executes rapidly on Moth Atlas cloud simulation servers for fast 2–5s turnaround.
3. **Local Quantum Simulator (`local`)**: Client-side seeded PRNG simulator matching the exact state Hamiltonian with physical readout noise, allowing 100% offline play without consuming API credits.
